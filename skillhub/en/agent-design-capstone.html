Capstone: Designing an AI Agent End to End

Afternoon · Capstone ⏱ ~60 min

This is the final deliverable of the session. You will design a complete AI agent that operates on opcp-explorer, bringing together everything from the day: the seven components, a loop variant and stopping criteria, a multi-agent decision, and all five guardrail layers. The output is a filled, documented agent-design deliverable.

Learning Objectives

  • Produce a fully documented design for a functional, reliable, controllable agent
  • Justify every design choice against the day's concepts
  • Confirm the design covers all five guardrail layers

Prerequisites

  • Completed: the entire morning and afternoon tracks
  • Completed: the threat-modeling workshop
  • The opcp-explorer connectivity lab (to ground your agent's tools)

What You Will Build

A single agent-design document for an opcp-explorer agent of your choosing — for example a deploy-and-verify agent, a backup agent, or a cost-watchdog agent. Use the template and keep its headings.

Deliverable: complete deliverables/agent-design-template.md (or .fr.md). This is your final, submittable artifact.

Design Steps

  1. Goal & autonomy — state the goal and place the agent on the autonomy spectrum.
  2. Seven components — specify brain, short-term and long-term memory, tools, planner, execution loop, and guardrails.
  3. Loop variant & stopping criteria — choose ReAct / Plan-and-Execute / Reflexion and define explicit stops.
  4. Multi-agent decision — single or multiple agents? Justify with the decision checklist.
  5. Guardrails — a concrete control for each of the five layers.
  6. Responsible-AI alignment — map transparency, accountability, data protection, security, and controllability.

Rubric

Your design should satisfy each learning objective of the session:

  • Assistant vs agent and the right autonomy level — addressed in goal & autonomy.
  • Seven components and their roles — each specified.
  • The agentic loop (Think → Act → Observe) — loop variant chosen and justified.
  • Multi-agent pattern selection — decision made and justified.
  • Guardrails (all five layers) — a control for each, confirmed below.
  • Responsible-AI aligned with the OVHcloud AI Policy — mapping completed.

Self-Assessment: Design Covers Five Layers

Tick each layer once your agent-design document specifies a concrete control for it. All five are required to mark the capstone complete.

Future enhancement: an interactive SkillHub form that generates this design document from guided inputs is planned. For now, the markdown template is the canonical deliverable.
